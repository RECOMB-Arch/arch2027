### {{ site.iteration }} RECOMB Satellite Conference on Hardware Acceleration of Bioinformatics Workloads

{{ site.title }} {{ site.year }} is the {{ site.iteration | downcase }} edition of the RECOMB Satellite Conference on Hardware Acceleration of Bioinformatics Workloads. It will take place in {{ site.location }} ({{ site.dates }}), as a satellite of [RECOMB {{ site.year }}](https://recomb.org/recomb{{ site.year }}). The conference brings together researchers from the bioinformatics, computational biology, and computer architecture communities to advance the acceleration of bioinformatics analysis (e.g., genome, transcriptome, and proteome analysis) through hardware-software co-design, hardware accelerators, and software systems tailored for new hardware, including compiler optimizations, APIs, and DSLs.

Keynote speakers, the call for papers, and key dates will be announced soon. For more about our history, see our [history page](https://recomb-arch.github.io/history/) or our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@recomb-arch).
